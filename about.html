About ExamSathi

EExamSathiStudent Portal
Student Portal

One simple place for exam-related information.

ExamSathi is designed to make government-exam updates, study resources, current affairs, mock tests and useful student tools easier to find.

What ExamSathi provides

• Latest Jobs and recruitment information
• Notifications and important notices
• Admit-card and result links
• Exam directory and study resources
• Current Affairs and quick revision
• Original practice mock tests
• Useful document and image tools

Our approach

We aim to keep information organized, easy to understand and linked to official sources whenever possible. Users should always verify important details such as eligibility, dates, fees and instructions on the official recruiting authority website.

Important

ExamSathi is an independent information portal. It is not an official government website and is not affiliated with SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NTA, Assam Government or any other recruiting authority unless specifically stated.
