🎯 Government Exam Directory

ExamSathi lists major central and Assam state exam categories. Always verify dates and eligibility on the official recruiting body's website.

🇮🇳 UPSC

Civil Services (CSE), Indian Forest Service (IFoS), NDA, CDS, CAPF, Engineering Services (ESE), Combined Medical Services (CMS), Geo-Scientist and other UPSC recruitments.

📋 SSC

CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, CPO, JE, Stenographer Grade C & D, Junior Hindi Translator and Selection Post.

🚆 Railways

RRB/RRC exams including NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician, JE, Paramedical, Ministerial & Isolated Categories and RPF recruitment.

🏦 Banking & Finance

IBPS PO/Clerk/SO/RRB, SBI PO/Clerk/SO, RBI Grade B/Assistant, NABARD and other major banking/regulatory recruitments.

🎓 NTA & Education

JEE, NEET-UG, UGC-NET, CUET and other major examinations conducted through NTA or national education bodies.

🛡️ Defence

Army, Navy, Air Force and Coast Guard recruitment/examinations, including NDA, CDS and AFCAT categories.

👩‍🏫 Teaching

CTET, TET and major central teaching recruitments such as KVS/NVS, subject to the current recruitment cycle.

🛡️ Insurance

Major insurance-sector recruitment such as LIC and public-sector insurance AO/Assistant categories.

🏛️ Regulatory Bodies

RBI, SEBI, NABARD and other regulatory/public financial institution recruitment examinations.

🟢 Assam PSC (APSC)

APSC Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) and other recruitment examinations for Assam Government services.

🟢 Assam Direct Recruitment / SLRC

State Level Recruitment Commission recruitment for major Class III and Class IV posts, including ADRE-related cycles.

👮 Assam Police

Constable, Sub-Inspector and other Assam Police / allied recruitment categories announced by the competent recruitment board.

📚 Assam TET

Assam TET and Special TET categories for eligible teaching aspirants.

🏥 Assam Health / DHS

Directorate of Health Services and related Assam health-department recruitment categories.

⚙️ Assam Engineering Service

Engineering recruitment conducted through the Assam Engineering Service Recruitment Board (AESRB).

⚖️ Gauhati High Court

Recruitment/examination categories notified by Gauhati High Court and its subordinate judiciary.

🏫 ASSEB / School Exams

HSLC/AHM and other school-board examination services and notices from ASSEB.

🌳 Assam Forest

Forest and environment-related recruitment categories when notified by the competent Assam authority.
